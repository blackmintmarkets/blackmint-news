Tether Buys $10 Million of Bitdeer Shares, Lifting Stake to 17.9%

An SEC filing shows Tether bought 3.7 million Bitdeer shares at about $2.69 on Nov. 25. The miner's stock closed at $17.83 in mid-June.

Tether bought 3,715,943 shares of bitcoin miner Bitdeer Technologies Group on Nov. 25 for about $10 million, lifting its stake to 41,445,453 Class A shares, or 17.9%, according to a Schedule 13D/A filed Friday with the SEC.

The purchase puts the issuer of USDT, the largest stablecoin with about $173.6 billion in circulation per DefiLlama, deeper into a Nasdaq-listed mining stock whose shares closed at $3.05 that day, down from $17.83 on June 12, per Yahoo Finance data.

Two Trades on One Day

The filing lists two open-market purchases by a Tether unit: 12,240 shares at $2.65 and 3,703,703 shares at $2.693. Together they cost about $10.01 million.

Tether's share count rose about 9.8% from the 37,729,510 shares it reported in June. Its percentage fell, though, from 19.7% to 17.9%, because Bitdeer had 231,794,894 Class A shares outstanding on Sept. 30, up from about 191 million used in the earlier filing, Crypto Briefing and crypto.news noted.

What a 13D Filing Tells Investors

SEC rules require anyone who gains beneficial ownership of more than 5% of a company's voting shares to file a Schedule 13D, according to the SEC's Investor.gov. Some passive holders can file a shorter 13G instead. Any material change requires a prompt amendment, called a 13D/A. This one is Amendment No. 14 to a filing first made on June 6, 2024.

The reporting parties are Tether Global Investments Fund, its subsidiary Tether International and Giancarlo Devasini. They share voting and selling power over the shares. At the Nov. 25 close, the full stake was worth about $126 million, by Black Mint's calculation from the filing and Yahoo Finance prices.

Bitdeer shares were at about $3.12 at 11:49 a.m. ET Friday, per Yahoo Finance, in a session that ends early at 1 p.m. ET after Thanksgiving.

In our opinion: The number that matters is $2.69, the price Tether paid for shares that closed at $17.83 in mid-June. A stablecoin issuer adding to a mining stock near its six-month low ties more of its own balance sheet to bitcoin mining, and at Wednesday's close the whole stake was worth about $126 million. The next marker is Bitdeer's share count: if it keeps growing, Tether's 17.9% shrinks again unless it buys more.
