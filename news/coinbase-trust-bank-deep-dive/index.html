Coinbase Opens a Federal Trust Bank That Can't Take Deposits

Custody, not deposits: the OCC wants $60 million of capital and its sign-off before new services. Coinbase's active users fell to 7.4 million last quarter.

Coinbase now owns a bank, though not the kind most people picture. Coinbase National Trust Company opened Nov. 9 under a federal charter that lets it hold crypto for clients but not take deposits or make loans, the OCC's Nov. 6 approval letter shows.

The business it will take over is large. Coinbase held $376.6 billion of customer crypto and stablecoins on Sept. 30, its quarterly filing shows. Neither the letter nor Coinbase's Nov. 9 announcement says how much of that will move into the bank, or when.

What a trust bank can and can't do

A national trust bank is a fiduciary: it holds assets for clients and has a legal duty to put their interests first. It takes no insured deposits, so there is no FDIC coverage. Client cash sits in "for benefit of" accounts at other banks, the OCC's April letter says.

For someone who buys bitcoin in the Coinbase app, nothing changes on day one. The charter covers custody "primarily for institutional clients," in the OCC's words.

Three years of conditions

For the bank's first three years, the final letter requires:

  • At least $60 million of tier 1 capital, with half of it or $30 million, whichever is greater, in cash-like assets
  • A separate reserve covering 180 days of operating expenses
  • A written OCC non-objection before any significant change, including "any offering of transactional services for custody account customers"

Who earns what

Coinbase Custody Trust Company, the New York-chartered firm that serves Coinbase Prime clients today, signed a Sept. 1 agreement to hand those accounts to the bank over time. Staking, Prime Trading and Prime Financing stay with Coinbase affiliates, so their fees land outside the bank. Neither letter lists custody fees.

Rates, deposits and a market slump

The Federal Reserve held its target range at 3.75% to 4% on Oct. 28, its statement shows. Rates reach Coinbase through USDC: lower average rates cut its third-quarter stablecoin revenue by $52.8 million from a year earlier, while bigger USDC balances added $84.6 million, the filing says.

Dollar stablecoin supply fell to $287.05 billion on Saturday, the lowest since September 2025, DefiLlama data show. The final letter requires the bank to conform, cease or divest activities if needed to meet the GENIUS Act, the 2025 stablecoin law. The April letter says the bank doesn't plan to issue stablecoins.

The slump shows in Coinbase's numbers. Monthly transacting users fell to 7.4 million in the third quarter from 9.3 million a year earlier. Bitcoin was near $64,970 at 3:47 p.m. ET Sunday, per CoinGecko, after dipping below $63,000 on Saturday night.

Who else holds one, and who objects

Anchorage Digital got there first. It won OCC approval on Jan. 13, 2021, and calls itself the first federally chartered digital asset bank. On Dec. 12, 2025, the OCC conditionally approved Circle's First National Digital Currency Bank, Ripple, BitGo, Fidelity Digital Assets and Paxos. Ripple won final approval on Oct. 26.

The Independent Community Bankers of America sued the OCC on Oct. 2, Reuters reported, challenging its March chartering rule and Protego Holdings' approval. "Congress did not create the national trust charter as a side door into the banking system for crypto firms," ICBA chief Rebeca Romero Rainey said. The OCC wrote in April that it "has the capability to resolve an uninsured national bank." Rick Schonberg, the bank's CEO, wrote in Coinbase's Nov. 9 post: "Custody is foundational to our business."

In our opinion: "bank" is accurate, but the letter describes a custodian on a three-year leash. Its first test arrives in a weak market, with fewer active users and a stablecoin business tied to Fed rates. The number to follow is how much of the $376.6 billion moves into the bank, and Coinbase hasn't published a schedule. The ICBA case matters too, since a ruling against the March rule would reach every crypto trust bank the OCC has approved.
