Kraken Says Trades Stand After Bitcoin Hit C$24,250 on CAD Pair

Kraken's BTC/CAD market fell about 72% below its later level overnight. Kraken found no breach; Blockstream's Adam Back blamed its USD/CAD market.

Bitcoin traded as low as C$24,250 on Kraken's Canadian-dollar market early Saturday, about 72% below the C$85,400 the same pair showed at 11 a.m. ET. Kraken said every executed trade will stand.

The exchange said heavy selling hit its CAD pairs starting around 3 p.m. ET Friday and pushed prices "well below other markets." In a post from Kraken Support at 6:12 a.m. ET Saturday, it said: "Our platform processed every order correctly, and our security teams found no sign of a breach or compromised accounts. Executed trades stand."

The slide took about 12 hours

Kraken's public price data show the BTC/CAD pair's hourly low falling from C$61,880 at 4 p.m. ET Friday to C$36,700 at 1 a.m. Saturday, then to C$24,250 in the 3 a.m. hour. About 77 bitcoin changed hands on the pair in that hour. By 5 a.m. the low was back above C$68,000. CryptoSlate also reported the C$24,250 low.

Over the same hours, Kraken's BTC/USD market bottomed at $55,100.

One explanation, not yet confirmed by Kraken

Adam Back, chief executive of Blockstream, said on X that the cause was Kraken's USD/CAD market, where he said the "local spot FX market-maker ran out of money" and the rate moved away from the market rate of 1.434. Kraken's statement did not give a cause or say who was selling.

How a local price can break away: each trading pair has its own order book (the list of open bids and offers). If buyers on one pair run thin, sellers fill against lower and lower bids. Traders can normally buy the cheap coin and sell it elsewhere, which pulls prices back together, but only as fast as they can move Canadian dollars in and out.

As of 11:50 a.m. ET, Kraken had not published a cause for the gap or said who was selling.
