Bitcoin ETFs Shed $430.2 Million, Erasing Wednesday's Inflow

Ether funds lost another $99.9 million the same day. Fidelity's FBTC led the bitcoin exits at $199.9 million, Farside shows.

U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs posted a net $430.2 million outflow on Nov. 5, wiping out the prior day's $74.6 million inflow and marking the largest single-session exit since Oct. 30, Farside Investors data show.

Bitcoin traded near $75,690 around midnight ET Friday, down about 6.0% over 24 hours, per CoinGecko. Rough day for the wrappers.

One session undid Wednesday.

Fidelity led the redemptions

Fidelity's FBTC accounted for $199.9 million of the exit. BlackRock's IBIT lost $105.0 million. Grayscale's GBTC shed $49.9 million, VanEck's HODL $23.6 million, Franklin's EZBC $32.9 million and ARK 21Shares' ARKB $18.9 million. No listed fund printed a creation on the day, Farside's table shows.

Spot bitcoin ETFs are funds that hold bitcoin in custody and trade on stock exchanges. Authorized participants create shares when investors buy and redeem them when investors sell. Net redemptions show up as outflows; a day with zero creations and several large redemptions prints a deep negative total.

U.S. spot ether ETFs also bled cash, with a $99.9 million net outflow on Nov. 5, per Farside's ether tracker. BlackRock's ETHA led with a $75.2 million exit; Bitwise's ETHB lost $22.0 million. The same funds had taken in $53.0 million on Nov. 4.

Since Oct. 30, the bitcoin group has now recorded five outflow sessions and one modest inflow.

Thursday alone beat Wednesday's whole recovery.

In our opinion: the number that matters is the two-day flip. A $74.6 million creation on Wednesday became a $430.2 million redemption on Thursday, a swing of about $505 million. Ether funds moved the same way, from plus $53.0 million to minus $99.9 million. Friday's print will show whether Thursday was a one-day exit or the start of another multi-day outflow streak.

Sources: Farside Investors (Bitcoin ETF flows) · Farside Investors (Ethereum ETF flows) · CoinGecko
