Binance Wins In-Principle Approval for Asia Hub in Bhutan's Gelephu

It isn't a full licence yet: Binance must meet Gelephu Central Bank's conditions first. The exchange holds licences in 15 markets, Reuters reported.

Binance has cleared the first regulatory step toward an Asia hub in Bhutan. Gelephu Central Bank granted the exchange in-principle approval to run regulated digital-asset operations in Gelephu Mindfulness City, Binance said in a statement dated Nov. 28.

It isn't a licence to trade yet. Binance "must satisfy the conditions specified by GCB and complete the remaining regulatory requirements before the relevant regulated activities may commence," the statement said. Reuters carried the same caveat.

How Gelephu's two-step licence works

Gelephu Mindfulness City (GMC) is a special administrative region in southern Bhutan with its own financial rulebook. Under the Gelephu Financial Services Act 2025, crypto businesses sit under the same rules as banks and brokers, and running an order-book exchange (a venue that matches buyers and sellers) needs a Financial Services Licence, the GMC licensing FAQ says.

An in-principle approval is the halfway mark. The FAQ says it doesn't allow any regulated activity until conditions are met, such as setting up a local company and opening a bank account inside the zone. Neither Binance nor GMC said which conditions apply in this case or when a full licence might follow.

Hiring and training in Bhutan

Binance and GMC also signed a cooperation agreement at Binance Blockchain Week 2026. The exchange said it plans a "substantive operational presence" in Bhutan, with local hiring and training in compliance and operations. It gave no headcount or investment figure.

Bhutan isn't new ground for the company. Binance launched a crypto payment system for tourists there in 2025, the statement said.

An expansion push after the October hack

Binance holds operating licences in 15 markets and is in talks with "at least five or six markets," Asia-Pacific head SB Seker told Reuters. The approval comes a month after hackers took an estimated $6.3 billion from the exchange on Oct. 29. Black Mint reported Nov. 26 that Binance had finished restoring withdrawals.

In our opinion: an in-principle approval opens the door only partway. GMC's own FAQ puts the usual wait for this stage at six to eight months after an application, and the full licence still sits on the other side of conditions nobody has published. The document worth waiting for is that Financial Services Licence, because until it lands, no Binance customer can trade a thing through Gelephu.
