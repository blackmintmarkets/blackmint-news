Nasdaq Falls 5.3% and Turns Negative for 2026 as Bitcoin Nears $59,000

The Nasdaq composite fell 5.28% Friday and the S&P 500 dropped 3.7% in an AI stock sell-off. Bitcoin traded at $58,970 and ether at $1,617 at 5:54 p.m. ET.

The Nasdaq composite fell 5.28% on Friday, Nov. 20, to close at 22,617.73, leaving it down 2.7% for the year, CNBC reported. The S&P 500 dropped 3.7% to 6,910.39, and Reuters and AP reported the same declines.

Where the selling hit

Artificial intelligence stocks led the drop. Nvidia fell about 14%, and Anthropic, which listed on Nasdaq a day earlier, closed down about 9%, according to Reuters and CNBC. CNBC said the S&P 500 and the Dow both closed 10% below their records. That is what traders call a correction: a 10% fall from a recent high.

Fund managers quoted by Reuters said they still back the AI trade. "Right now the market is selling first and asking questions later," Michael Monaghan of Founder ETFs told Reuters.

Crypto in the same session

Bitcoin traded at $58,970 at 5:54 p.m. ET, down 5.1% in 24 hours, and ether was at $1,617, down 8.1%, according to CoinGecko. Bitcoin dipped below $58,000 earlier in the day, a high-to-low drop of 31.8% from its Oct. 21 peak, The Defiant reported. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index, which scores market mood from 0 to 100 using price swings, volume and other data, read 12, or "Extreme Fear."

When stocks fall sharply, funds that cut risk can sell crypto along with shares. That is one way crypto and tech stocks can end up moving together, as they did Friday.

In our opinion: the number to hold onto is 22,617.73, because it puts the Nasdaq's entire 2026 gain back on the table in one session. Crypto didn't decouple; bitcoin and ether both fell with tech, a reminder that in a broad sell-off it trades with the risk crowd. Monday's open, and whether the S&P 500 holds its correction level, is the next test for both markets.
