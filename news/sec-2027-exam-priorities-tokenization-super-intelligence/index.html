SEC Puts Tokenization and 'Super Intelligence' on Its 2027 Exam Priority List

The SEC's exam division named tokenization and advanced AI, including 'super intelligence,' among its focus areas for fiscal 2027.

The Securities and Exchange Commission's Division of Examinations released its priorities for fiscal year 2027 on Friday, and two items stand out for crypto and fintech firms: tokenization and advanced artificial intelligence.

What is on the list

The SEC said examiners will pay closer attention to risks tied to evolving market infrastructure, naming Treasury clearing reforms and tokenization. The list also includes emerging technologies, specifically "super intelligence" and other advanced tools that could affect market operations, compliance, fraud prevention and investor protection.

Other focus areas include investment advisers' duties to clients, protection of retail investors in complex or illiquid products, private funds and private credit, broker-dealer anti-money laundering compliance, and cybersecurity. The division oversees more than 34,000 registrants, including advisers, broker-dealers and clearing agencies.

"The markets we oversee continue to evolve rapidly, and the examination program must evolve with them," said Keith Cassidy, director of the Division of Examinations. SEC Chairman Paul Atkins called examinations one of the agency's most important tools for promoting compliance.

The SEC stressed that the list is not exhaustive and may change during the year as markets shift. Decrypt also reported on the inclusion of tokenization and AI.

Why it matters

Exam priorities do not create new rules, but they signal where regulators will look first. Firms that tokenize stocks, funds or Treasuries, a fast-growing area, can expect more questions about custody, settlement and disclosures. Naming tokenization as an infrastructure topic also shows the SEC treating it as part of mainstream markets rather than a niche experiment.
