Variational Raises $110 Million at $1.5 Billion Valuation, Led by Bain

The on-chain derivatives platform says its Omni app has handled $475 billion in volume since early 2025, with more than half now in stocks and commodities.

Variational, an on-chain derivatives platform, said Wednesday it raised $110 million in a Series B round led by Bain Capital Crypto at a $1.5 billion valuation, according to a company release. Dragonfly, HSG and Peak XV also took part, Reuters reported.

The round more than doubles the $50 million Series A that Dragonfly led in May.

What Omni Does

Variational runs Omni, a trading platform offering leveraged bets on more than 550 crypto and traditional markets, including stocks, indexes, commodities and currencies. A derivative is a contract whose value tracks another asset, so a trader can bet on gold or an index without owning it.

Omni charges no trading fees. Instead, an in-house liquidity provider quotes every price and earns the spread (the gap between the buy and sell price), the company said. That provider pulls prices from crypto exchanges and traditional firms, including what Variational called "a tier-1 market maker."

The Traction Numbers

Since launching in early 2025, Omni has processed more than $475 billion in volume and holds more than $1.3 billion in open interest, the value of all open positions, the company said. Reuters carried the same figures. Variational said more than half of daily volume is now in traditional markets, and that it has more than 100,000 accounts.

The company said the money will lift open-interest caps that traders keep hitting and add hundreds of new markets, with 24/7 swaps trading and a trading API planned.

Open Questions

All traction figures come from Variational and have not been independently audited. The release does not say which countries can use Omni or how its stock and commodity contracts are regulated. Leveraged trading can multiply losses as well as gains.

In our opinion: The company is betting that traders want stock and oil exposure on crypto rails, and the volume split suggests some already do. The risk sits with the in-house liquidity provider on the other side of every trade, so how it is capitalized matters more than the valuation. Watch whether Variational publishes audited figures and says where Omni is licensed.
