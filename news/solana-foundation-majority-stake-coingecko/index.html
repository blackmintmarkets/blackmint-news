Solana Foundation Takes Majority Stake in CoinGecko, Plans Trading on the Site

The Solana Foundation now holds a majority interest in CoinGecko, which has 18 million registered users. Neither side disclosed the price or the exact stake.

The Solana Foundation now holds a majority interest in CoinGecko, the crypto price and data site with 18 million registered users, the foundation's chief product officer, Vibhu Norby, said Tuesday. Neither side disclosed the price or the exact size of the stake, according to CoinDesk and Unchained, via Yahoo Finance.

The deal was announced at the foundation's Breakpoint conference in London. CoinGecko's own announcement says it is "backed by Solana Foundation" and lists three focus areas: data and API services, market research tools, and trading, which it describes as a capability it is "exploring."

What changes for users

The foundation said it plans "on-platform trading powered by Solana" on CoinGecko's website, its GeckoTerminal tracker and its mobile app, Unchained reported. Norby also pointed to wider coverage of stocks and tokenized real-world assets, meaning shares, bonds and other traditional assets recorded on a blockchain.

According to the foundation, CoinGecko logs more than 50 million monthly sessions, and about 180,000 apps and businesses pull its prices through its API, a feed that lets other software request data automatically. CoinGecko said its website, app and API "will continue operating as usual" and that its methods for ranking and pricing coins will stay public.

CoinGecko was founded in 2014 by Bobby Ong and TM Lee. In January, CoinDesk reported that the company had hired the bank Moelis to explore a sale at a possible valuation near $500 million, citing unnamed people familiar with the matter. Binance bought rival CoinMarketCap in 2020.

CoinGecko and the foundation did not respond to CoinDesk's request for comment on the deal terms. Black Mint uses CoinGecko for the prices in its stories. Solana's SOL traded at $71.96 at 11:55 a.m. ET, up 1.3% in 24 hours, according to CoinGecko.

In our opinion: CoinMarketCap and CoinGecko now both sit under owners with a stake in what they rank: one an exchange, the other a blockchain's foundation. The promise to keep methods public is the part worth holding them to, because thousands of apps treat those prices as neutral. The first test comes when Solana trading goes live on the site: watch whether rankings, listings and default pages stay chain-neutral.
