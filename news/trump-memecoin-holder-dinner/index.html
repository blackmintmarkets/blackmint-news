Trump Hosts Dinner Tonight for 185 Top Holders of TRUMP Memecoin

The token trades near $0.89, down 98.8% from its January 2025 high. Two Trump-linked companies own 80% of its supply, the coin's official site says.

President Donald Trump is scheduled to host the top 185 holders of his TRUMP memecoin at a dinner Sunday night at Trump National Golf Club outside Washington, with the token trading 98.8% below its January 2025 peak.

The event's official page lists Trump as featured speaker, along with boxer Floyd Mayweather, investor Kevin O'Leary and NBA champion Tristan Thompson. The top 29 holders get a VIP reception, and the page says, "There will be no private meetings with The President. No gifts will be accepted."

How seats were handed out

A memecoin is a token built around an internet meme or a famous likeness. Seats went to registered holders ranked by time-weighted holdings, so the score rises with how many tokens a wallet held and for how long. The scoring window ran from Sept. 30 to a final snapshot on Nov. 16, according to the event page and The Block.

It is the third event for top holders. The first dinner was held at the same club in May 2025, and a holder event took place at Mar-a-Lago in April 2026, Al Jazeera and Reuters and The Block reported.

Who holds the supply

TRUMP traded at $0.8863 at 5:49 p.m. ET, up 0.2% over 24 hours, against an all-time high of $73.43 set on the evening of Jan. 18, 2025, ET, according to CoinGecko. That puts the value of tokens now in circulation near $250 million.

The TRUMP Coin Club site says CIC Digital LLC, a Trump Organization affiliate, and Fight Fight Fight LLC together own 80% of the planned 1 billion-token supply, which unlocks over three years. The site says the token is not intended to be "an investment opportunity."

Criticism and the White House response

Eight Democratic senators raised ethics concerns about the event in an October letter, The Block and Al Jazeera reported. The Democratic National Committee also criticized the dinner. The White House has said the president's attendance at a privately organized event does not make it a government function, according to both outlets.

In our opinion: The stake is concentrated. Two Trump-linked companies own 80% of TRUMP's planned supply, so no other holder has as much riding on the token's price. Next on the calendar: a new Congress with Democratic majorities takes its seats in January, and the club's Super Bowl package follows on Feb. 12-14, 2027.
