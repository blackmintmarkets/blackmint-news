Galxe Shuts Quest, ID and Passport, Sets Dec. 15 Reward Deadline

Galxe says it served 36 million users. Campaigns end Dec. 5, and staked G and Smart Savings balances will get a separate claim process later.

Galxe users have until Dec. 15 to claim any campaign rewards. After that, the whole Galxe website goes read-only, the company said on X at 1:38 a.m. ET Monday as it folds its team and brand into Gravity, its own blockchain.

Galxe Quest, Galxe ID, Galxe Passport and related services are being wound down. The last day to take part in any campaign is Dec. 5.

How Galxe campaigns worked

Galxe ran quest campaigns for crypto projects: a project posted tasks, such as making an on-chain transaction or following an account, and users who finished them earned rewards. Galxe ID and Passport handled identity and credentials behind those campaigns. The company says it served 36 million users and more than 7,700 partner projects over five-plus years.

What stopped Monday

Some of it ended right away. Starting Monday, Spaces (the project pages) can no longer create new campaigns, and new Galxe+ and Galxe Score subscriptions and Passport minting are closed, the post says.

Gravity, the network, keeps running, and the company said there are "no changes to the G token as part of this transition." G is the gas token on Gravity L1, Crypto Briefing reported.

Some balances wait for a second notice

Three kinds of assets are not on the December timetable: Smart Savings balances, staked G not yet withdrawn, and G held in wallets on Gravity Alpha Mainnet. Galxe says these "remain safe" and will follow a separate claim process, with details in a later post on X. It gave no date for that post.

That older network is already gone. Gravity Alpha Mainnet shut down on Nov. 1 after a bridging window that ran Oct. 23-25, Crypto Briefing reported, and Galxe had warned users on X in October to move funds off the network before those deadlines.

Galxe's post does not say how many users still hold unclaimed rewards or how much those rewards are worth.

The next dates are fixed: campaigns close Dec. 5, and reward claims close Dec. 15.
