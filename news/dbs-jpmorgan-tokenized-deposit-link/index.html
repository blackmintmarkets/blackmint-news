DBS and JPMorgan Link Tokenized Dollars for 24/7 US-Singapore Moves

Ant International is the first client. Transfers into and out of JPMorgan's blockchain accounts still pause for three hours every Saturday.

DBS Bank and JPMorgan's Kinexys unit launched a service on Monday that lets institutional clients move tokenized U.S. dollars between Singapore and the U.S. 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Ant International is the first client.

For a corporate treasurer, that means cash sitting in Singapore can reach a U.S. subsidiary after the usual bank cut-off times instead of waiting for Monday morning.

A bank deposit wearing a token

A tokenized deposit is an ordinary bank balance recorded as a token on the bank's own blockchain. It is still a dollar claim on DBS or JPMorgan, not a new cryptocurrency. Both networks are permissioned, so only approved banks and clients can use them, unlike Bitcoin or Ethereum, where anyone can join.

The link joins DBS Token Services with Kinexys's Blockchain Deposit Account network. Partior, a settlement company both banks helped found, coordinates settlement, the DBS release says. It puts into use a framework the two banks announced in November 2025, and DBS calls the setup "industry-first."

"Tokenised money is moving rapidly from experimentation to delivering live tangible value for clients," said Rachel Chew, DBS's chief operating officer and co-head of digital assets for global transaction services.

The asterisk on "24/7"

Transfers inside the Kinexys network run around the clock. Moving money between a traditional JPMorgan account and a blockchain account still stops from 3 p.m. to 6 p.m. ET each Saturday. The release says a fix is in development.

What the banks left out

The announcement gives no fees, transfer limits or dollar volumes so far. To size the opportunity, the banks cite a Money20/20 and FXC Intelligence forecast that Asia's outbound cross-border payments will grow from $13.5 trillion in 2025 to $24 trillion by 2033. In September, DBS and Citi reported a weekend dollar payment between the same two countries using tokenized deposits over Swift's Digital Ledger, The Business Times noted.

In our opinion: the prize here is corporate cash that used to sit still from Friday night to Monday morning, and Ant International has signed up first. The next number to look for is client two, along with any fee schedule. Until that Saturday pause goes away, the old banking calendar still gets three hours a week.
