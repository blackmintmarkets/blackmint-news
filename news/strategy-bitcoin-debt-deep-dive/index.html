Strategy's Debt and Preferred Stock Cost $1.43 Billion a Year

Its 850,849 bitcoin cost $75,452 each, above Monday's price. A $4.75 billion cash reserve covers about 40 months of interest and dividends.

Strategy needs about $1.43 billion a year to cover interest on its convertible notes and dividends on five preferred stocks, its Nov. 15 investor briefing shows. Its 850,849 bitcoin sat below cost Monday.

How the borrowing works

A convertible note is a loan the lender can swap for stock at a set price, so it carries a low coupon. Perpetual preferred stock never matures. It pays a fixed dividend ahead of common shareholders, and if it's "cumulative," a skipped payment piles up and must be paid before common holders get anything.

850,849 coins bought over six years

The company paid $64.20 billion for its coins, or $75,452 each including fees, its Nov. 16 8-K shows. It held 70,469 at the end of 2020 and 447,470 at the end of 2024, earlier filings show. With bitcoin at $64,922 at 3:42 p.m. ET Monday, per CoinGecko, the stack was worth about $55.2 billion, roughly $9 billion under cost. That loss stays on paper unless coins are sold.

$6.71 billion of notes, mostly at 0%

Six series were outstanding on Sept. 30, the quarterly filing shows. Each lets holders demand cash on a put date:

  • $1.01 billion at 0.625%, due 2028, put Sept. 15, 2027, converts at $183.19
  • $1.5 billion at 0%, due 2029, put June 1, 2028, converts at $672.40
  • $2 billion at 0%, due 2030, put March 1, 2028, converts at $433.43
  • $800 million at 0.625%, due 2030, put Sept. 15, 2028, converts at $149.77
  • $603.7 million at 0.875%, due 2031, put Sept. 15, 2028, converts at $232.72
  • $800 million at 2.25%, due 2032, put June 15, 2029, converts at $204.33

Coupons cost about $34.6 million a year. MSTR traded near $103.32 at 3:43 p.m. ET, per Yahoo Finance, below every conversion price.

The preferred bill is the big one

Rates are from the 10-Q; the STRC count subtracts weekly buybacks since Oct. 25:

  • STRC: 12%, cumulative, paid daily; about 77.6 million shares, roughly $932 million a year
  • STRD: 10%, not cumulative; $140.2 million
  • STRF: 10%, cumulative; $128.4 million
  • STRK: 8%, cumulative, payable in cash or stock; $112.2 million
  • STRE: 10% in euros, cumulative; €77.5 million, about $85 million

With coupons, that's about $1.43 billion, matching Strategy's figure.

Where the money comes from

Software won't cover it. Third-quarter revenue was $125.4 million, and the 10-Q says software cash isn't expected to meet the company's needs.

The USD Reserve held $4.75 billion on Nov. 15, plus $151.2 million of USD Cash, the 8-K says. Board policy keeps at least 12 months of payments in it. At $1.43 billion a year, the reserve alone covers about 40 months. Strategy puts both pools at 3.42 years.

New stock fills the rest. Strategy sold $244.7 million of MSTR shares last week and has $18.09 billion of sales capacity left.

It has also sold bitcoin: about 6,947 coins this year for $431.8 million, to fund dividends and STRC buybacks, the 10-Q and its Oct. 29 earnings release show.

mNAV and a falling bitcoin

mNAV is Strategy's share price divided by its bitcoin per share after debt and preferred claims, net of cash. It was 1.09x at Nov. 13 prices, the briefing and strategy.com show. Our recalculation at Monday afternoon prices gives about 1.1x.

Near or below 1x, new MSTR sales add less per share. The briefing warns that issuance "below appropriate value can reduce Net BPS." A deeper bitcoin slide thins the cushion behind the preferred. STRC traded as low as about $71 in June, the 10-Q says.

Rates set the price of money

The Federal Reserve held its target range at 3.75% to 4% on Oct. 28, its statement shows, and meets again Dec. 8-9. STRC pays about eight points over that. Interest on Strategy's cash paid for $21.8 million of last week's STRC buybacks. Rate cuts would shrink that income.

In our opinion: the yearly bill looks covered for now, with about 40 months of reserve and no new stock sold. The harder stretch starts in September 2027, when holders can demand cash for $1.01 billion of notes, then $4.9 billion more in 2028. Every series converts above Monday's share price. The number to check each Monday is mNAV in the 8-K and on strategy.com, since it decides whether new stock or bitcoin sales pay the bill.
