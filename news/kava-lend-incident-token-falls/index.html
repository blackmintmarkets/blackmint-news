KAVA Falls 41% as Kava Lend Incident Pauses Exchanges and a Bridge

Kava says the event is contained in Kava Lend and is not an infinite mint. Its Axelar bridge withdrawals are paused while it prepares an incident report.

The KAVA token fell 41.5% in 24 hours to about $0.0397 at 11:58 a.m. ET on Saturday, per CoinGecko, after an incident the Kava team says is confined to Kava Lend, a lending app built on its network.

That leaves KAVA's total market value near $43 million, CoinGecko data show. For now, KAVA on partner exchanges can't be deposited or withdrawn, Kava says.

What Kava has said so far

Kava first posted at 6:28 a.m. ET that it was tracking "unexpected behavior" from an app in its ecosystem. About half an hour later it said it was aware of on-chain activity involving the bridging and sale of KAVA tokens on BNB Smart Chain (BSC).

"This is not an infinite mint," the team wrote. "The event is isolated and contained within Kava Lend." It said partner exchanges had paused deposits and withdrawals as a standard precaution.

At 9:05 a.m. ET, Kava said it had paused the Axelar bridge, so cross-chain withdrawals through it are on hold. The team said a full incident report would follow.

How a bridge carries tokens to another chain

A bridge moves a token from one blockchain to another. In a lock-and-mint design, it holds the original coins on the home chain and issues matching tokens on the other one, such as BSC. Once tokens cross, they can be sold on that chain's trading venues within minutes. Pausing the bridge stops more of them from crossing.

What is not yet known

Kava has not said how many tokens were involved, how the problem in Kava Lend worked, or whether any user deposits were lost. Kava has not called the event a hack. Black Mint is not reporting third-party estimates of the token amount until Kava or an auditor confirms them.

In our opinion: the incident report is the document that matters here. Users need three answers from it: how many KAVA left through the bridge, whether Kava Lend depositors lost anything, and when the exchange and bridge pauses end. Until it lands, Kava's official account is the place to check for updates.
