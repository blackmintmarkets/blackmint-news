Schroders Puts an Emerging-Market Bond Fund on Solana

Schroders, which manages more than $1 trillion, is offering its emerging-market bond strategy on Solana through Centrifuge, its first deployment on that chain.

Schroders, the London asset manager with more than $1 trillion under management, launched a tokenized emerging-market bond fund on Solana on Wednesday through Centrifuge, a Centrifuge post said. It is the firm's first deployment on that network.

This one is not a cash fund. Most tokenized funds so far hold Treasuries or money market assets. This one holds bonds from governments and companies in developing economies. "Emerging markets debt introduces a fundamentally different source of risk and return," Centrifuge's Anil Sood said, as CoinDesk reported.

How the token works

Buyers do not hold the Schroders fund directly. They get SISF tokens in a British Virgin Islands feeder fund, which in turn owns shares of Schroder International Selection Fund Emerging Market Bond, a Luxembourg fund, according to Centrifuge's fund page. Token values track that fund's net asset value. Subscriptions and redemptions are made in stablecoins, and redemptions settle three business days later.

The doors are narrow. The fund is open only to non-U.S. professional investors, with a minimum of 100,000 USDC. The page lists underlying fund fees of 1.29% and says the feeder has no operating history of its own.

Schroders has managed emerging-market debt for more than 50 years. Marita Mcginley, its head of operations for Asia-Pacific and digital assets, said tokenizing the strategy lets the firm reach "a new generation of investors." Solana carries more than $4 billion in tokenized real-world assets, Centrifuge said.

In our opinion: Moving riskier credit onto a public chain is a bigger step than parking cash there, and it tests whether onchain buyers want yield with real default and currency risk. The early size is small. Watch whether SISF tokens get accepted as collateral in Solana lending apps, because that is where tokenization stops being a wrapper and starts being plumbing.
